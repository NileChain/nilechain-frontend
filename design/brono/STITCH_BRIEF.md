## PASTE INTO STITCH (after uploading the screenshots)

```text
PROJECT
NileChain — Egyptian B2B platform that connects farms and food factories: AI matching, trust score (0–100, HIGH = safer), Arabic contracts with dual signing, fulfillment (ship / weigh / QC / gate rejection), in-platform wallet hold & release, and disputes. Not a bank, not crypto, not farm ERP.

TASK
Design a PUBLIC LANDING PAGE (Arabic, RTL) that feels world-class and opens the chest — the confidence of Stripe, the typographic discipline of Linear, the tonal drama of Vercel/Mercury — but the subject is Egyptian agricultural trade (soil, trucks, weighbridges, contracts).

REFERENCE IMAGES (I uploaded them)
Treat the uploaded screenshots as MANDATORY craft references:
- Steal: scale contrast (huge display type vs tiny labels), asymmetric / editorial composition, product UI overlapping photography, one dark full-bleed anchor section, hairline rules, micro-detail density, motion-ready hierarchy.
- Do NOT steal: their logos, their copy, their purple/black cyber aesthetic, or their product domains. Re-express those craft moves for NileChain.

VISUAL MANDATE
- First viewport only contains: brand NileChain (LTR wordmark, never mirrored, never “ChainNile”), ONE Arabic headline at dramatic scale (~90–120px desktop), ONE short supporting line, TWO CTAs (انضم كمزرعة / انضم كمصنع), and ONE product glimpse (match + trust score as a typographic hero number).
- No trust-bar icon strip under the hero. No fake stats (“10k farms”). No partner logo row. No floating glass cards on blurred stock.
- Photography: art-directed Egyptian agri world, one color grade across the page, aggressive crops, UI overlapping photo edges with hard borders.
- One dark full-bleed section for trust score anatomy or Arabic contract honesty.
- Paper / film texture, not gloss. Warm off-white canvas + deep agrarian green + sand/terracotta as rare accent.
- Decision numbers as graphics: 78 trust, 40 risk threshold, 30/70 payment, match weights — huge tabular numerals.
- Vary section archetypes — never three identical equal cards in a row.
- Typography: authoritative Arabic display + clean Arabic body (e.g. Cairo / Tajawal or better pairing). English only where needed for brand.

ANTI-PATTERNS (reject these)
Generic green SaaS template, centered stacked sections, Inter-looking Latin defaults everywhere, purple/indigo gradients, robots/AI brains, sparkles, crypto chains, glassmorphism, clay 3D icons, “unlock the future of agriculture” copy.

OUTPUT
High-fidelity desktop landing, Arabic RTL, then show mobile nav collapse. Make it look designed by a senior human who would put this in a portfolio — not an admin theme with stock photos.
```
